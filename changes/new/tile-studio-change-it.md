- **Point at a part of a tile and say what to change.** In Tile Studio, tap **Point at something**, tap the light or the reason line in the preview, and say what should change (bigger, move up, a different color). Notes show as numbered pins, and **Copy change request** sends the tile and each note to a chat in one message. The answer fills the form as before, and **Back** now goes back up to 5 fills.
