- **Editable lists, right where the list lives.** A list kept on this dashboard (Setup → Lists, "This dashboard") can now be filled in and ticked off in two places, no other app or account needed: on the **List tile** (type in the box and tap +, tap a line to tick it off, × to delete) and in **Controls → Lists**, right under the list's own settings, with the same items, ticks and an Add box. A new **Clear the ticks every day at 00:00** option on the list itself turns it into a daily checklist: the ticks are cleared overnight while the items stay, and an **Untick all** button does the same reset on demand. Lists kept in another app stay read-only on the dashboard as before, and the phone page at `/list` keeps editing the first dashboard list. Items are stored per list in `work/lists.json`. A tile can also hide its Add box to show just the items — tick things off on the screen, edit the items in Controls → Lists.
